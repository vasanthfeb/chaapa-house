About

At Chaapa House, we believe that aquariums do more than beautify a space they bring peace, life, and a touch of nature into your world. With a passion for aquatic life and years of experience in aquarium care, design, and maintenance, we’ve created a one-stop destination f or all your aquarium needs.

Founded with a deep love for ornamental fish and underwater ecosystems, Chaapa House, now proudly operates in two major cities: Coimbatore and Bengaluru. Whether you're a first-time hobbyist or a seasoned aquarist, our friendly team is here to guide you every step of the way.
